Kotilingeshwara.

A distinctive spiritual destination known for its extraordinary collection of Shiva lingas and the giant Shiva statue.

What to explore

Explore the temple complex and its many Shiva lingas, along with the large Shiva statue. Dress respectfully and follow site instructions.
