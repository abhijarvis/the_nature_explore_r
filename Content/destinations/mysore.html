Mysore.

A relaxed mix of royal heritage, food, markets and nearby nature, making Mysore an easy weekend road trip from Bengaluru.

Region
Mysuru, Karnataka
Known for
Palace · Heritage
Best for
Weekend · Culture
Trip style
2 days

What to explore

Build your visit around Mysore Palace, Devaraja Market, Chamundi Hill and the city's food culture. Evening palace illumination is a popular experience when available.

Must-see

Mysore Palace and Chamundi Hill.

Travel tip

Book accommodation early for major festival and holiday weekends.

Route & map

Bengaluru → Ramanagara → Mandya → Mysore

2-day plan

Day 1: Drive to Mysore, explore the palace and central city.

Day 2: Chamundi Hill, markets and a relaxed return to Bengaluru.
