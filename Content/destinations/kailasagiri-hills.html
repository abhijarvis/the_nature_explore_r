Kailasagiri Hills.

A hilltop escape overlooking Visakhapatnam and the Bay of Bengal, with broad coastal views and landscaped surroundings.

Region
Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh
Known for
Sea views · Hilltop
Best for
Views · Photos
Trip style
1 day / city stop

What to explore

Enjoy the hilltop views, landscaped park and the coastline stretching around Visakhapatnam. It works especially well around sunrise or sunset.

Highlight

Panoramic Bay of Bengal and city views from the hill.

Travel tip

Combine with other Visakhapatnam coastal attractions if making the long trip from Bengaluru.

Route & map

Suggested plan

Reach Visakhapatnam, explore Kailasagiri around golden hour, then continue to the city's coastal viewpoints and return to your stay.
