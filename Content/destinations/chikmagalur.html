Karnataka · Western Ghats · Coffee country

Chikmagalur.

A coffee-country escape of misty hills, plantations, waterfalls and mountain roads. Mullayanagiri rises to 1,930 m, making it the highest peak in Karnataka.

Why go

Chase clouds above the coffee estates.

Chikmagalur works especially well for a two- or three-day road trip. You can combine a sunrise mountain drive with coffee-estate experiences, waterfalls and short hikes.

Must see

Build your mountain loop.

Mullayanagiri

Karnataka's highest peak at 1,930 m. Go early for cooler weather, mountain light and better visibility.

Baba Budangiri

A dramatic hill range with coffee history, viewpoints and a natural extension to a Mullayanagiri drive.

Jhari Falls

A popular waterfall experience where local transport may be useful for the final approach.

Hebbe Falls

A two-tier waterfall inside the forested hills. Access conditions and permits can vary, so check locally.

Kudremukh

A biodiversity-rich mountain region with trekking opportunities and forest landscapes.

Coffee Estates

Slow down with a plantation walk, estate stay, tasting or café stop around the coffee-growing belt.

Route & map

Bengaluru → Chikmagalur

A practical road-trip route is Bengaluru → Tumakuru → Hassan → Chikmagalur. The drive is roughly 250 km and typically takes around 4.5–5.5 hours depending on traffic.

Suggested plan

2–3 days in Chikmagalur.

Day 1 · Coffee + town

Settle into coffee country.

Drive from Bengaluru, check in and spend the afternoon around a coffee estate or local café. Keep sunset flexible around the hills.

Day 2 · High hills

Mullayanagiri + Baba Budangiri.

Start before sunrise, drive through the mountain roads and combine Mullayanagiri with Baba Budangiri and nearby viewpoints.

Day 3 · Optional

Waterfall or forest day.

Choose Jhari/Hebbe Falls or extend toward the Kudremukh side depending on access, weather and your energy level.

Slow option

Stay on the estate.

Skip the packed checklist and spend a morning walking the plantation, tasting coffee and photographing the mist.

Road-trip notes

Before you ride.

🌧️ MonsoonRain can make hill roads, waterfalls and forest trails slower or temporarily restricted. Check local conditions.
🌄 SunriseMullayanagiri is best treated as an early-start stop if you want cooler conditions and clearer views.
🥾 Forest trailsProtected areas may require permits or guided access. Confirm the current rules before trekking.
