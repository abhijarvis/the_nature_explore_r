Coorg.

Known as Kodagu, Coorg blends coffee plantations, misty hills, waterfalls and winding roads. Madikeri is the district headquarters, with elevations across the region of roughly 900–1,750 m.

Why go

Ride where coffee meets clouds.

Coorg is made for a relaxed road trip: scenic plantation roads, viewpoints, waterfalls and enough variety to build either a packed weekend or a slow three-day escape.

Must see

Make your Coorg circuit.

Madikeri

Use Madikeri as a base for Raja's Seat, Madikeri Fort, Omkareshwar Temple and nearby coffee-country drives.

Abbey Falls

A popular waterfall stop surrounded by greenery and coffee-growing country near Madikeri.

Mandalpatti

Head into the hills for sweeping Western Ghats views. Local jeep access can be useful on rough sections.

Dubare

Combine the river-side setting with an elephant-camp experience and a slower day outside Madikeri.

Raja's Seat

A classic Madikeri viewpoint, especially suited to an evening stop when the hills catch the last light.

Tadiandamol

At 1,748 m, it is Coorg's highest peak and a strong option for an offbeat trekking day.

Suggested plan

2–3 days in Coorg.

Day 1 · Madikeri

Town + viewpoints.

Drive in from Bengaluru, settle into your stay, explore Madikeri and finish at Raja's Seat. Keep the evening unhurried.

Day 2 · Hills + falls

Mandalpatti & Abbey Falls.

Start early for the hills, return toward Madikeri and add Abbey Falls or a coffee-estate experience depending on time.

Day 3 · Optional

Go deeper into Kodagu.

Add Dubare, a plantation stay/experience or Tadiandamol if your group is prepared for a full trekking day.

Food stop

Try Kodava flavours.

Look for local specialities such as pandi curry, akki roti and kadumbuttu, while checking the current menu at your chosen restaurant.

Road-trip notes

Before you ride.

🌧️ RainMonsoon conditions can make forest and hill routes slower. Check local access before heading to waterfalls or trekking areas.
🏍️ Scenic roadsAllow extra time for bends, photo stops and slower traffic through villages and plantation areas.
🥾 TrekkingFor Tadiandamol and other trails, verify current local rules, access and weather before setting out.
