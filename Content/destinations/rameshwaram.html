Rameshwaram.

A memorable long-distance road trip combining coastal landscapes, pilgrimage heritage and the dramatic Pamban crossing.

Region
Ramanathapuram, Tamil Nadu
Known for
Temple · Coast · Pamban
Best for
Road trip · Heritage
Trip style
2–3 days

What to explore

Visit Ramanathaswamy Temple, the Pamban area, Dhanushkodi and the coastal stretches around the island. Keep extra time for sunrise and sunset.

Must-see

Pamban Bridge area, Dhanushkodi and the shoreline.

Road-trip tip

Plan fuel and breaks carefully on the long approach from Bengaluru; coastal weather can be windy and hot.

Route & map

Bengaluru → Salem → Madurai → Ramanathapuram → Rameshwaram

Open route in Google Maps ↗

Simple 2-day plan

Day 1: Reach Rameshwaram, explore the temple and Pamban area.

Day 2: Start early for Dhanushkodi and the coast, then return.

Good to know: Follow local access rules around sensitive coastal areas and check weather before heading to exposed stretches.
