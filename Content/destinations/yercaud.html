Yercaud.

A cool hill escape above Salem, known for misty roads, viewpoints, a lake, coffee and forested hills. Yercaud sits at about 1,515 m above sea level.

Why go

Take the road into the hills.

Yercaud is a strong choice for a short road trip from Bengaluru. The destination combines winding ghat roads with easy-to-reach viewpoints and a relaxed hill-town pace.

Must see

Build your Yercaud loop.

Yercaud Lake

Start around the Big Lake for boating, a relaxed walk and easy access to nearby attractions.

Pagoda Point

Head east for broad views toward the plains. It is around 5 km from Yercaud town.

Killiyur Falls

A waterfall close to the lake area. Flow is especially worth checking after the monsoon.

Lady's Seat

A classic viewpoint overlooking the plains and the winding ghat road below.

Shevarayan Temple

A cave temple on the hills and one of Yercaud's notable cultural stops.

Botanical Garden

A quieter stop for plant lovers, photography and a slower part of the itinerary.

Suggested plan

2 days in Yercaud.

Day 1 · Drive + viewpoints

Ride up, slow down.

Start early from Bengaluru → Salem → Yercaud. Check in, explore Yercaud Lake, then ride to Pagoda Point and finish around Lady's Seat for evening views.

Day 2 · Nature loop

Waterfalls + hills.

Begin with the Botanical Garden and Shevarayan Temple. Add Killiyur Falls when conditions are suitable, then take a relaxed lunch and descend before dark.

Road-trip notes

Before you ride.

🌧️ MonsoonExpect wet roads and lower visibility. Check local conditions before waterfall or ghat-road plans.
🏍️ Ghat roadsKeep speeds conservative on bends and avoid riding after dark if you are unfamiliar with the route.
📍 NavigationDownload your route or keep offline navigation available because hill coverage can vary.
