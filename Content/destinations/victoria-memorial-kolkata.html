Victoria Memorial.

An iconic Kolkata landmark surrounded by landscaped grounds, combining architecture, history and a peaceful city stop.

What to explore

Explore the memorial, museum collections and surrounding gardens. The monument is especially photogenic in the early morning and around golden hour.

Highlight

The distinctive white marble architecture and landscaped setting.

Travel tip

Check museum opening days, ticketing and photography rules before visiting.
