Nandi Hills.

One of Bengaluru's classic sunrise rides, with winding approach roads, viewpoints and cool hilltop air.

Location
Chikkaballapur district
Known for
Sunrise · Views
Best for
Bike · Day trip
Trip style
Half / full day

What to explore

Enjoy the hill approach, sunrise viewpoints, Tipu's Drop area and the historic hilltop surroundings. Weekends can be busy, so an early start makes the ride more enjoyable.

Ride highlight

Early-morning climb with sweeping views over the plains.

Travel tip

Check current entry timings and traffic conditions before leaving Bengaluru.

Route & map

Bengaluru → Yelahanka → Devanahalli → Nandi Hills

Open route in Google Maps ↗

Simple plan

Early morning: Ride to the hilltop for sunrise.

Morning: Explore viewpoints and heritage spots.

Return: Breakfast around Chikkaballapur/Devanahalli and head back.
