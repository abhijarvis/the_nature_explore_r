Devarayanadurga Hills.

Rocky hill roads, forested slopes, temples and wide views make DD Hills a rewarding short ride from Bengaluru.

Type
Hill & forest escape
Region
Tumakuru, Karnataka

Sunrise · Ride · Views

What to explore

Explore Devarayanadurga, the hilltop temple areas, forest roads and viewpoints around the hill. Keep time for a relaxed ride through the surrounding countryside.

Ride highlights

Curvy approach roads, rocky landscapes and elevated viewpoints.

Travel tip

Start early for cooler weather and better light. Avoid risky roadside parking on bends.

Route & map

Bengaluru → Nelamangala → Tumakuru → Devarayanadurga

Simple day plan

Morning: Ride from Bengaluru and explore the hills.

Afternoon: Lunch around Tumakuru and a relaxed return.

Good to know: Weather can change quickly on exposed hill sections; carry water and keep your ride gear comfortable.
